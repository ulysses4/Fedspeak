How the score works

relevance = |D stance − R stance| × data-center weight × office multiplier × competitiveness. Stance runs −2 (champions binding AI regulation or data-center moratoria) to +2 (champions AI infrastructure or federal preemption); data-center weight runs 0 (none built or planned) to 3 (top-tier hub); office multiplier is 1.0 for Senate and governor, 0.5 for House; competitiveness is 1.0 toss-up, 0.8 lean, 0.5 likely, 0.2 solid, so a safe-seat result is treated as already priced.

The sign comes from who wins. An AI-restrictive candidate losing in a buildout state is a tailwind for AI-exposed equities; winning is a headwind. If both nominees score the same, the race is neutral however it breaks. The running tally sums the signed relevance of called races only; a chamber-control overlay is applied in the written projection, not the tally. This is a scenario read, not investment advice. Stances are sourced from votes, bills and statements linked in the table; the full working doc with the state-by-state data-center map is here.
